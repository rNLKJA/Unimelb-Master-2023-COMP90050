# Reading list

The ten papers Group 40 read for the survey. The PDFs used to live in `intro/` and `indexing/`; they are the publishers' and authors' copyright, so this list links to them instead. Every one is cited in the report (`../presentation/`) and appears on the website's survey map.

## Self-driving databases (`intro/`)

1. Andrew Pavlo, Gustavo Angulo, Joy Arulraj, Haibin Lin, Jiexi Lin, Lin Ma, Prashanth Menon, Todd C. Mowry, Matthew Perron, Ian Quah, Siddharth Santurkar, Anthony Tomasic, Skye Toor, Dana Van Aken, Ziqi Wang, Yingjun Wu, Ran Xian and Tieying Zhang. _Self-Driving Database Management Systems._ CIDR 2017. <https://dblp.org/search?q=Self-Driving%20Database%20Management%20Systems%20Pavlo%20CIDR>
2. Jan Kossmann and Rainer Schlosser. _Self-driving database systems: a conceptual approach._ Distributed and Parallel Databases 38:795–817, 2020. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10619-020-07288-w>
3. Andrew Pavlo, Matthew Butrovich, Lin Ma, Prashanth Menon, Wan Shen Lim, Dana Van Aken and William Zhang. _Make Your Database System Dream of Electric Sheep: Towards Self-Driving Operation._ PVLDB 14(12), 2021. <https://doi.org/10.14778/3476311.3476411>
4. Lin Ma. _Self-Driving Database Management Systems: Forecasting, Modeling, and Planning._ PhD thesis, Carnegie Mellon University, technical report CMU-CS-21-134, August 2021. <https://dblp.org/search?q=Self-Driving%20Database%20Management%20Systems%3A%20Forecasting%2C%20Modeling%2C%20and%20Planning>

## Index selection (`indexing/`)

5. Sanjay Agrawal, Surajit Chaudhuri and Vivek Narasayya. _Automated Selection of Materialized Views and Indexes for SQL Databases._ VLDB 2000. <https://dblp.org/search?q=Automated%20Selection%20of%20Materialized%20Views%20and%20Indexes%20for%20SQL%20Databases>
6. Gary Valentin, Michael Zuliani, Daniel C. Zilio, Guy Lohman and Alan Skelley. _DB2 Advisor: An Optimizer Smart Enough to Recommend Its Own Indexes._ ICDE 2000. <https://dblp.org/search?q=DB2%20Advisor%3A%20An%20Optimizer%20Smart%20Enough%20to%20Recommend%20Its%20Own%20Indexes>
7. Sudipto Das, Miroslav Grbic, Igor Ilic, Isidora Jovandic, Andrija Jovanovic, Vivek R. Narasayya, Miodrag Radulovic, Maja Stikic, Gaoxiang Xu and Surajit Chaudhuri. _Automatically Indexing Millions of Databases in Microsoft Azure SQL Database._ SIGMOD 2019. <https://dblp.org/search?q=Automatically%20Indexing%20Millions%20of%20Databases%20in%20Microsoft%20Azure%20SQL%20Database>
8. Ryan Marcus and Olga Papaemmanouil. _Towards a Hands-Free Query Optimizer through Deep Learning._ CIDR 2019. <https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.10212>
9. R. Malinga Perera, Bastian Oetomo, Benjamin I. P. Rubinstein and Renata Borovica-Gajic. _No DBA? No Regret! Multi-Armed Bandits for Index Tuning of Analytical and HTAP Workloads With Provable Guarantees._ IEEE TKDE, 2023. <https://doi.org/10.1109/TKDE.2023.3271664>
10. Wentao Wu, Chi Wang, Tarique Siddiqui, Junxiong Wang, Vivek Narasayya, Surajit Chaudhuri and Philip A. Bernstein. _Budget-aware Index Tuning with Reinforcement Learning._ SIGMOD 2022. <https://doi.org/10.1145/3514221.3526128>
